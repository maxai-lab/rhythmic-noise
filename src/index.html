Rhythm control for accent conversion

TTS engines generate natural, intelligible speech from text, but typically offer limited control over word timing and pauses. Voice and accent conversion systems preserve source timing more directly, but can retain source characteristics such as accent. They can also introduce word insertions or substitutions, reducing intelligibility and increasing word error rate (WER).

Rhythmic Noise bridges this gap by using the source rhythm to give a TTS engine temporal control. The samples below demonstrate accent conversion while preserving intelligibility and temporal fidelity to the source.
